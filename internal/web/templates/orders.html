Before you rely on a dispatch

Know the limits

  • Stocks under surveillance may reject API orders when broker or exchange consent is required.
  • An order may fail because of funds, holdings, quantity, lot or freeze size, circuit or LPP limits, product eligibility, or other broker and exchange checks.
  • Orders dispatch beginning at {{.DispatchTime}} IST. Queueframe sends each order once and does not retry an order-placement request.
  • Trader login is unavailable between {{.BlackoutStart}} and {{.BlackoutEnd}} IST.
  • Zerodha documents limits of ten order requests per second and 400 per minute per client account. Reorder the queue to choose which orders enter each batch.
  • You must complete Kite login between 06:00 and {{.BlackoutStart}} IST on the execution date for that date’s orders to be active.
Delivery sells and DDPI

A delivery sell may fail without DDPI or separate same-day CDSL TPIN authorisation. Queueframe does not perform either flow.

  1. Open Zerodha Console.
  2. Go to Account → Demat.
  3. Select Enable DDPI and continue to e-sign.
  4. Complete Aadhaar OTP verification and signing.

A planned request time is not a promise of OMS acceptance, exchange receipt, matching, or execution.
